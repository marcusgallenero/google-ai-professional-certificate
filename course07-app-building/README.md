# Course 7: Energy Consumption Analyzer

## 📌 Project Overview
Built an interactive app that transforms dense hourly consumption files (.csv) into simple energy graphs. 
Offers custom energy-saving recommendations, and calculates how much money a user can save.

_Access the App [Here](https://ai.studio/apps/da6c49ed-389f-44f4-860e-b3265c3ed917?fullscreenApplet=true)_

---

## 🛠️ Problem & Solutions
**The Problem:**
Hourly energy consumption graphs are vague and unclear as they lack context. 
The general audience cannot easily comprehend the data on them.  

**The Solution:**
Vibe-coded with **Google AI Studio** to create an app to contextualize hourly energy consumption data, and provide relevant insights.

---

## 📁 Featured Files:
- 'index.html': The operational interface and calculations engine generated via AI Collaboration
- 'design-prompts.md': log detailing prompts used to instruct AI and resolve errors
- 'sample-energy-data.csv': FirstEnergy hourly energy consumption accessible [here](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/robikscube/hourly-energy-consumption?resource=download&select=FE_hourly.csv)
