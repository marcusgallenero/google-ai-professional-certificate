# Course 3: AI Powered Drone Research Hub

## 📌 Project Overview
Built a specialized **AI Research Hub** using **Google NotebookLM** to summarize, analyze, and organize drone flight organization

--- 
## 🛠️ Problem & Solution
**The Problem:** 
Standard AI tools, like AI chatbots often make up facts, or guess when they don't know the answer (hallucination).
This can lead to faulty or inaccurate research, further leading to ineffective solutions.

**The Solution:**
Execute a method of **AI Grounding** by uploading official flight specs onto NotebookLM.
This forces the AI to look strictly at the verfied facts, completely eliminating hallucinations from AI.

---
## 📁 Folder Contents
1. **'/source-documents'**: Technical data sheets and other guides on autonomous drone flight used to train AI workspace.
2. **'/ai-outputs'**: Final summaries and guides generated by the AI, showing how drones calculate obstacle avoidance and navigate when GPS is disconnected

---
## 💡 Key Takeaways
By completing this project, I demonstrated how to:
- Transform hours of dense and confusing technical manuals into clear and practical engineering summaries
- Train an AI model to stay accurate by grounding it strictly to trusted sources
- Bridge the gap between complex autonomous hardware and smart AI tools
